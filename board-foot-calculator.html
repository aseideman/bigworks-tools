    Board feet are figured on nominal dimensions, which is how lumber is sold and priced. A 2×4 is counted as 2" × 4" even though it measures 1½" × 3½". Hardwood is usually sold in quarters of an inch of thickness: 4/4 is 1", 5/4 is 1¼", 6/4 is 1½", 8/4 is 2".
